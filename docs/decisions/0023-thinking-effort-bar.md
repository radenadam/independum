# Thinking effort bar

- Status: superseded by [decision 0028](0028-thinking-effort-list.md)
- Date: 2026-10-27

## Context

Decision 0022 opened the thinking effort as a large dial. Even after it shrank, the maintainer felt the dial was off, and asked for an upright bar with the level named above it.

## Decision

- The thinking effort menu is an upright bar, and the menu is only as wide as its content. The chosen level sits above the bar in orange. The bar runs from the lowest level at its foot to the highest at its top, with a dot for each level that the model offers, and it fills in orange from its foot up to a round knob.
- A drag or a click on the bar chooses the nearest level, and the arrow keys move it, with Up for more effort. The bar takes the focus when a keyboard is likely, and on a phone the menu opens as a sheet like the other menus.
- The Effort icon on the button stays the dial that [decision 0022](0022-thinking-effort-dial-menu.md) describes, without words and with its tooltip.

## Consequences

- Decision 0022 is superseded, except for the Effort icon.
- A pop-up can now be as wide as its content, besides the regular and narrow widths.

# Harness engineering and two halves

- Status: accepted, amends [decision 0057](0057-versions-and-checkpoints.md), [decision 0071](0071-repository-healing.md), and [decision 0075](0075-control-parity-and-newcomer-journeys.md)

- Kind: product requirement and working preference

## Context

A configurable default agent does not define Independum's difference. Research, development, teaching, automation, teams, and assistants need different workflows. The older checkpoint combines engineering readiness with newcomer usability and fixes the tool inventory to today's six.

## Decision

Independum is a harness engineering tool, made easy and transparent. Judge a market by how easily people can engineer it into their workflow.

The first half makes the harness engineering tool ready. Every part can be engineered. The default profile works immediately after model connection, with minimal tools and a full agent loop. Universal tools, Script, and a replaceable loop are in scope.

The screen structure redesign stays in this checkpoint for people who know agents, starting with the maintainer. Keep its seven journeys. Tailoring language, guidance, and learning material for people new to agents follows in the shipping half.

The shipping half makes a competitive agent and packages release. It includes coding-agent parity, more tools, extensive docs, the GitHub README, website, and memory across chats. Version naming from decision 0057 does not shrink engineering acceptance.

The principles are easy without a cliff, exact transparency, and real modular removal. The coordinator's delegated meanings of easy are effect to cause, progression through screen, files, scripts and code, safe experiments, fast feedback, plain-file sharing, self-explanation, and few consistent concepts. Effect to cause and safe experiments distinguish easy engineering from mere possibility.

Running elsewhere is direction only. Channels get separate profiles, tools, and permissions and never inherit local account authority.

The maintainer keeps product meaning, audience, and first encounters. The coordinator owns technical and architectural choices and must serve Independum in every aspect. This corrects compulsory maintainer planning for every technical tool choice.

## Consequences

[The product definition](../product/README.md) owns current intent. [The checkpoint](../checkpoint.md) separates implementation from evidence and keeps CP-24 for the narrowed redesign. [The roadmap](../roadmap.md) orders contracts first and shipping afterward. This replaces the newcomer acceptance of decisions 0071 and 0075, while keeping their parity and comprehension requirements for the intended reader.

The foundation is settled product intent. Technical protocol design is the next work, not a reason to reopen these answers.

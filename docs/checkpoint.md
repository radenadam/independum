# Harness engineering checkpoint

This checkpoint makes Independum ready as a harness engineering tool. It does not complete the shipping half. [The product definition](product/README.md) owns that boundary. Implementation and verification are separate. No row is complete. A merged slice, native skip, or unrun scenario cannot close acceptance.

The baseline is the 2026-11-10 source audit of `947520b`, which ran no tests. Historical test counts are not current evidence. R05 means direct maintainer answers of 2026-11-05, R09 the 2026-11-09 restatement, and A10 the 2026-11-10 answers. F15 means direct maintainer product answers of 2026-11-15, reconciled in decisions 0081 through 0086. D15 means choices delegated to the coordinator that day. The kernel is coordinator judgment accepted by the maintainer. Private evidence stays outside this ledger.

S means screen, F files, H headless. All covers applicable workflows under [control parity](product/README.md#visibility-and-control). Implementation is partial, missing, blocked, or complete. Verification is unverified, partial, or verified. Complete requires verified acceptance. Linked area pages retain implementation details and limits.
